### - Advances in Agentic Computer Graphics

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 119 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01860)

**초록**:

Computer graphics is built on code. Large language model (LLM) agents can now generate graphics code and iteratively refine it through various feedback. This capability opens the door to a potentially new paradigm of computer graphics, with graphics algorithms increasingly built around agentic systems to automate progressively complex tasks. Over the past years, research on agentic graphics has expanded rapidly across a diverse range of tasks. This rapid growth makes it increasingly difficult for manual literature reviews to keep pace with new developments. At the same time, similar ideas are often described using different terminology across these tasks, further complicating efforts to develop a systematic understanding of the field. In this report, we address these challenges through two contributions. First, we introduce a unified taxonomy for characterizing agentic systems across diverse computer graphics tasks. Second, we develop an agentic literature system that can assist human researchers in analyzing existing and newly published papers at scale. Building on these contributions, we analyze emerging research trends and discuss open challenges in coordinating agents and tools across graphics tasks, designing interfaces and representations for human-agent collaboration, sustaining long-horizon planning and refinement, and reliably evaluating interactive graphics systems.

### - Wave Forcing: Towards Speed-of-Light Streaming Video Generation

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 117 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00859) | [Project](https://sjtu-deng-lab.github.io/WaveForcing/)

**초록**:

Real-time long-video generation requires continuous output with consistent appearance and natural motion. Existing chunk-wise autoregressive methods face challenges in both generation efficiency and long-video quality: serialized denoising and history-cache refresh require two forward passes even with one-step denoising, and longer rollouts can degrade quality and cause camera drift. We propose Wave Forcing (WF), which co-designs generation, training, and execution for continuous video output with multi-step denoising. WF uses chunk-wise causal attention to access earlier, cleaner chunks before their denoising is complete, and distributes staggered denoising and cache-refresh stages across full-model replicas to achieve 1F1C: one output chunk per concurrent forward round after pipeline fill. To adapt the model to this continuous generation process, we develop a three-stage training procedure that successively introduces mixed-noise denoising, motion supervision, and causal adaptation using self-generated history. To execute this process efficiently, Wave Runtime (WaveRT) uses Wave Parallelism (WP) for early K/V delivery and communication-computation overlap, together with optimized kernels and pipelined VAE decoding. Experiments on Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B show that 5-step WF maintains overall quality comparable to Rolling Forcing while achieving higher Dynamic Degree on both short and long videos. Our system efficiency experiments show that 4-step WF achieves 147.6 DiT FPS and 134.8 end-to-end FPS on eight H200 GPUs, yielding 7.07x the single-GPU end-to-end throughput. In DiT-only tests with matched SageAttention+W8A8 backends, denoising steps, and GPU counts, WP delivers up to 2.53x the throughput of the faster of the Ulysses and Ring context-parallel baselines across six model scales (1.3B-14B).

### - CUA-Master: Scaling Computer-use Agents through Open-World Recursive Improvements

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 95 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03126) | [Project](https://xlyu0106.github.io/CUA-Master/)

**초록**:

Despite recent advances in CUAs, their development remains constrained by the limited diversity, controllability, and reliability of interaction data. Moreover, conventional optimization pipelines lack iterative feedback, while existing recursive improvement approaches often rely on self-generated experiences, limiting ability expansion and yielding diminishing returns. To address these challenges, we introduce CUA-Master, a nested double-loop recursive improvement framework. At the system level, an outer world-data-model flywheel continuously incorporates open-world corpus and leverages evolving model weaknesses to guide subsequent data construction and optimization iterations. At the data level, an inner flywheel couples open-world seeding and autocatalytic propagation. The former mines authentic workflows as seed tasks from instructional videos, and employs visual-semantic dual-path trajectory reconstruction; The latter transforms these seeds into reusable primitives for controllable synthesis of diverse, complex, and verifiable tasks. To enable efficient and reliable trajectory collection, we further introduce a feedback-guided snapshot-rollback retry mechanism in various real environments. Extensive experiments demonstrate that CUA-Master-27B achieves competitive performance across GUI-only and GUI-CLI hybrid benchmarks, scoring 80.2 on OSWorld Verified, 36.9 on OSWorld 2.0, 43.9 on WeaveBench, and 25.0 on Agents' Last Exam. Our framework also substantially improves the quality and efficiency of data synthesis, and exhibits continuous gains across recursive iterations.

### - CoworkOS:Scaling Agent Coworkers for Real-world Automation

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 89 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03178) | [Project](https://coworkos-ai.github.io)

**초록**:

Rapid advances in cowork agents are shifting human-AI collaboration toward real-world automation, delivering software, designs, and other artifacts. However, such productivity work demands both high-quality delivery on complex productivity tasks and continued monitoring and decision-making over long horizons, a combination that remains an open challenge. We introduce CoworkOS, a general-purpose harness for long-horizon work that separates task strategy from execution management: the model decides actions, delegation, and acceptance, while the runtime maintains state and feedback. Its three-layer architecture enables direct execution or delegation to Subagents, synchronously or asynchronously, under stable responsibilities. Through a shared application layer, humans and agents inspect and revise the same evolving documents, presentations, CAD models, and Blender scenes, so human-in-the-loop participation can enter both through conversation and through direct modification of the work itself. To sustain continuity across agents and sessions, CoworkOS further introduces hierarchical workspaces and persistent semantic memory, so working files, key decisions, and long-running Job logs remain shareable, reusable, and resumable after interruption. Across 12 benchmarks and 6 categories of real-world applications, CoworkOS proves highly competitive with frontier agent applications such as Codex and DeepSeek Harness. As a sustained-work case study, CoworkOS uses GLM-5.3 as its backbone to conduct RSI-driven automated post-training on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B. Over five rounds, the model's average score across eight benchmarks rose from 33.19 to 42.95 (+9.76). Looking ahead, we believe CoworkOS opens a meaningful path toward general cowork-agent intelligence.

### - Video Generation Meets Agent Harness: Agentic Video Generation, Agentic World Modeling and Recursive Self-Improvement

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 72 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40191) | [Project](https://agentic-videogen.github.io/)

**초록**:

Video generation is shifting from end-to-end synthesis toward agentic generation, where planning, tool use, and feedback enable coherent, controllable multi-shot videos. In parallel, video world models provide action-conditioned observations and predicted futures, serving as interactive environments for agents. We study their intersection through the agent harness: an executable runtime that constructs context, exposes tools, maintains state, routes feedback, and manages budgets around video models. This report examines three directions. First, agentic video generation, where harnesses coordinate scripting, shot planning, asset management, production, and editing, alongside the evaluation challenges of these systems. Second, agentic world modeling, where harnesses mediate observation, control, execution, and state. We discuss hybrid designs in which game engines or agent-written programs maintain world state while video models render observations, and how agents and world models can mutually evaluate and improve one another. Third, recursive self-improvement (RSI) in video model development, distinguishing output revisions, updates to harness state, generator parameters, and evaluators, and changes to the improvement procedure itself. We identify how these improvements feed back into subsequent development. Across these directions, we analyze essential agent capabilities, including audiovisual perception, long-horizon planning, memory, code-based interaction, feedback-guided control, skill reuse, and multi-agent coordination. We conclude with challenges in autonomous video production, lifelong memory, programmable hybrid control, autonomous model development, provenance, cost accounting, and evaluation linking video quality and task performance to sustained improvements in models and harnesses.

### - GPTQ: Accurate Post-Training Quantization for Generative Pre-trained
  Transformers

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 13 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.17323)

**초록**:

Generative Pre-trained Transformer models, known as GPT or OPT, set
themselves apart through breakthrough performance across complex language
modelling tasks, but also by their extremely high computational and storage
costs. Specifically, due to their massive size, even inference for large,
highly-accurate GPT models may require multiple performant GPUs, which limits
the usability of such models. While there is emerging work on relieving this
pressure via model compression, the applicability and performance of existing
compression techniques is limited by the scale and complexity of GPT models. In
this paper, we address this challenge, and propose GPTQ, a new one-shot weight
quantization method based on approximate second-order information, that is both
highly-accurate and highly-efficient. Specifically, GPTQ can quantize GPT
models with 175 billion parameters in approximately four GPU hours, reducing
the bitwidth down to 3 or 4 bits per weight, with negligible accuracy
degradation relative to the uncompressed baseline. Our method more than doubles
the compression gains relative to previously-proposed one-shot quantization
methods, preserving accuracy, allowing us for the first time to execute an 175
billion-parameter model inside a single GPU for generative inference. Moreover,
we also show that our method can still provide reasonable accuracy in the
extreme quantization regime, in which weights are quantized to 2-bit or even
ternary quantization levels. We show experimentally that these improvements can
be leveraged for end-to-end inference speedups over FP16, of around 3.25x when
using high-end GPUs (NVIDIA A100) and 4.5x when using more cost-effective ones
(NVIDIA A6000). The implementation is available at
https://github.com/IST-DASLab/gptq.

### - Analyzing and Improving the Image Quality of StyleGAN

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 0 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.04958)

**초록**:

The style-based GAN architecture (StyleGAN) yields state-of-the-art results
in data-driven unconditional generative image modeling. We expose and analyze
several of its characteristic artifacts, and propose changes in both model
architecture and training methods to address them. In particular, we redesign
the generator normalization, revisit progressive growing, and regularize the
generator to encourage good conditioning in the mapping from latent codes to
images. In addition to improving image quality, this path length regularizer
yields the additional benefit that the generator becomes significantly easier
to invert. This makes it possible to reliably attribute a generated image to a
particular network. We furthermore visualize how well the generator utilizes
its output resolution, and identify a capacity problem, motivating us to train
larger models for additional quality improvements. Overall, our improved model
redefines the state of the art in unconditional image modeling, both in terms
of existing distribution quality metrics as well as perceived image quality.

### - ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 38 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629)

**초록**:

While large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated impressive capabilities
across tasks in language understanding and interactive decision making, their
abilities for reasoning (e.g. chain-of-thought prompting) and acting (e.g.
action plan generation) have primarily been studied as separate topics. In this
paper, we explore the use of LLMs to generate both reasoning traces and
task-specific actions in an interleaved manner, allowing for greater synergy
between the two: reasoning traces help the model induce, track, and update
action plans as well as handle exceptions, while actions allow it to interface
with external sources, such as knowledge bases or environments, to gather
additional information. We apply our approach, named ReAct, to a diverse set of
language and decision making tasks and demonstrate its effectiveness over
state-of-the-art baselines, as well as improved human interpretability and
trustworthiness over methods without reasoning or acting components.
Concretely, on question answering (HotpotQA) and fact verification (Fever),
ReAct overcomes issues of hallucination and error propagation prevalent in
chain-of-thought reasoning by interacting with a simple Wikipedia API, and
generates human-like task-solving trajectories that are more interpretable than
baselines without reasoning traces. On two interactive decision making
benchmarks (ALFWorld and WebShop), ReAct outperforms imitation and
reinforcement learning methods by an absolute success rate of 34% and 10%
respectively, while being prompted with only one or two in-context examples.
