### - Antom 3-in-1 Transformer: A Payment Foundation Model

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 106 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25403)

**초록**:

We report the development of Antom 3-in-1 Transformer, a payment foundation model that integrates sequence, tabular, and graph representations for payment risk detection. The model contains approximately 7B parameters, with approximately 1.7B activated per task route. Antom 3-in-1 Transformer is pretrained to learn reusable behavioral and relational representations, primarily on Ant International's internal payment data. In a cross-business transfer experiment, pretraining improves the target business metric by 34.5% over the same architecture trained from scratch. With only 25% of the training labels, the pretrained model outperforms the full-label scratch baseline in Average Precision (AP) and Recall@0.5% on different merchant domains. In a four-month online experiment on Ant International's billion-scale payment platform, the model reduced the overall loss rate by 37.88% relative to XGB-Online.

### - Lazy Agents Answer Against Their Own Beliefs: Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Beliefs Teaches Them to Act on What They Believe

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 98 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32884) | [Project](https://declare-lab.github.io/RLVB/?case=tabletop_cups&m=rlvb_grpo&s=0)

**초록**:

AI agents working on long tasks often commit to an answer before they have enough evidence, a behavior called laziness. In our evaluations, they frequently commit even while their stated belief still names several possible answers. An outcome reward at the end of the episode scores only the final answer, so it cannot teach the agent when to commit, because a lucky guess receives the same reward as an answer backed by evidence. During training, however, the simulator can compute the exact posterior over the answer at every step and tell whether the evidence is already enough. We propose Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Beliefs (RLVB), which rewards the agent for taking further steps while this posterior still allows several answers and for committing once the evidence is sufficient. The reward needs no judge, critic or reward model, and it never reads the belief line the agent writes before each action. We also introduce Pro Worlds, a benchmark of visual puzzles where the agent views a large world through a small window. We evaluate three vision-language models on three benchmarks, two of which are never seen in training. RLVB improves the base model in all nine settings, by 17.1% on average. Under the outcome reward, GRPO and PPO fall below the base model on average. RLVB also outperforms PPO with a process reward from an LLM judge by 9.3% on average while using 1.6 times fewer GPU-hours. The trained agent answers with sufficient evidence in 51%, 12% and 74% of the episodes on the three benchmarks, against 8%, 0.4% and 36% for the base model. Its accuracy gain therefore comes from answering more often at high levels of evidence. The belief line shows how the agent reaches this evidence: it acts on its own belief. It commits at only 0.1 to 2.2% of the turns at which the evidence is insufficient and its line still names several answers, against 3 to 68% for the base model.

### - Don't Sleep, Keep Watch: Proactive Perception for Computer-Use Agents

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 98 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28947) | [Project](https://sujq0722.github.io/NightWatch/)

**초록**:

Computer-use agents (CUAs) built on vision-language models are increasingly entrusted with long-horizon tasks in live desktop, web, and mobile environments, where the screen keeps changing on its own. However, current CUAs lack real-time situational awareness: they perceive the environment through discrete snapshots, and the substantial inference latency of VLMs, compounded by highly dynamic interfaces, opens a perception-action gap in which the observed state frequently becomes stale before the corresponding action is executed. To bridge this gap, we present NightWatch, a plug-and-play framework that endows any CUA with continuous, temporally grounded perception without changing the agent's model, prompt, or action space. NightWatch couples the VLM agent with NightWatch-0.8B, a lightweight System-1 model that performs streaming monitoring of the interface state and keeps the agent synchronized with the evolving environment at low computational cost and latency, thereby grounding each decision in the current state rather than an outdated snapshot and reducing both stale actions and idle waiting while preserving task success. Experiments on desktop and dynamic web environments (OSWorld-Verified and DynaCU-Bench) show that NightWatch, driven by a watcher of only 0.8B parameters, reduces non-reasoning wall-clock time by 33% and wasted time by 53% on OSWorld while keeping task success on par, raises success on DynaCU-Bench from 39.5% to 44.2% with the large VLM agent, and is more efficient than passive-watching baselines such as AOI and PUSV under the same screenshot-only input, as well as a System-1 decision model more than 30times its size. Overall, NightWatch demonstrates that the timing of perception is as critical as its content, marking a step toward agents that maintain continuous awareness of their environment rather than perceiving it turn by turn.

### - Rewirable Circuits in Transformers with Loom Attention

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 90 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30634)

**초록**:

We propose loom attention, which allows a Transformer to dynamically rewire its circuits at every token by interweaving a head's vertical attention over the outputs of earlier heads (to obtain its query, key and value) with its horizontal attention over tokens. We instantiate vertical attention efficiently with normalized and gated linear attention over a per-token matrix memory, which stores the compressed head outputs and serves as a second residual stream. This yields LoomFormer, a dual-stream architecture that splits control and content between the vector and matrix streams. Across model sizes and training token budgets in language modeling and downstream tasks, LoomFormer matches Transformers trained with over 3x compute and recent residual-stream baselines (HC/mHC, MUDDFormer, AttnRes) trained with ~1.7-2.8x. For example, trained on the same 300B tokens with only 2% extra FLOPs, LoomPythia-2.8B achieves ~1.6x the average downstream gain of mHC-Pythia-2.8B over Pythia-2.8B, and matches (0-shot) or surpasses (5-shot) Pythia-12B. Code is available at https://github.com/Caiyun-AI/LoomFormer

### - Nemotron-Labs-VisualCreation: Pixel as Code for Agentic Visual Creation

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 82 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31358) | [Project](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-Labs-Image-30B-A3B)

**초록**:

Visual creation demands expressive imagery together with precise control over text, layout, and structure. We introduce Nemotron-Labs-VisualCreation, an agentic framework that connects these capabilities through Pixel as Code. Raster images are represented by learned discrete visual tokens, while structured artifacts use explicit programs. A shared generation-and-rendering interface connects these representations to visual feedback across images, vector graphics, webpages, and documents. A Nemotron-native harness coordinates planning and raster candidate selection, with targeted repair for editable structured and hybrid artifacts. To support this interface, we develop Nemotron-Labs-Image-Tokenizer, the autoregressive raster generator Nemotron-Labs-Image-30B-A3B, and the structured-code model family Nemotron-Labs-VisionCode. On approximately 178K images spanning six visual domains, our tokenizer improves reconstruction PSNR from 26.08 to 29.57 dB over Emu3.5 IBQ. It also provides 7.13x faster encoding and 10.93x faster decoding at batch size one on A100. In standalone evaluation, one Nemotron-Labs-VisionCode variant surpasses Kimi K3.1 Pro on the aggregate across Chart2Code, MMSVG, Design2Code, and Image2Struct. At the system level, the Nemotron-native configuration raises GenEval2 Soft-TIFA GM from 31.71 to 52.56 using the same raster generator. With our best setting, Nemotron-Labs-VisualCreation achieves performance competitive with GPT Image 2 and Nano Banana Pro across most benchmarks with available comparisons.

### - GLU Variants Improve Transformer

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 5 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.05202)

**초록**:

Gated Linear Units (arXiv:1612.08083) consist of the component-wise product
of two linear projections, one of which is first passed through a sigmoid
function. Variations on GLU are possible, using different nonlinear (or even
linear) functions in place of sigmoid. We test these variants in the
feed-forward sublayers of the Transformer (arXiv:1706.03762)
sequence-to-sequence model, and find that some of them yield quality
improvements over the typically-used ReLU or GELU activations.

### - Squeeze-and-Excitation Networks

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 0 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.01507)

**초록**:

The central building block of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) is the
convolution operator, which enables networks to construct informative features
by fusing both spatial and channel-wise information within local receptive
fields at each layer. A broad range of prior research has investigated the
spatial component of this relationship, seeking to strengthen the
representational power of a CNN by enhancing the quality of spatial encodings
throughout its feature hierarchy. In this work, we focus instead on the channel
relationship and propose a novel architectural unit, which we term the
"Squeeze-and-Excitation" (SE) block, that adaptively recalibrates channel-wise
feature responses by explicitly modelling interdependencies between channels.
We show that these blocks can be stacked together to form SENet architectures
that generalise extremely effectively across different datasets. We further
demonstrate that SE blocks bring significant improvements in performance for
existing state-of-the-art CNNs at slight additional computational cost.
Squeeze-and-Excitation Networks formed the foundation of our ILSVRC 2017
classification submission which won first place and reduced the top-5 error to
2.251%, surpassing the winning entry of 2016 by a relative improvement of ~25%.
Models and code are available at https://github.com/hujie-frank/SENet.

### - DeBERTa: Decoding-enhanced BERT with Disentangled Attention

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 4 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.03654)

**초록**:

Recent progress in pre-trained neural language models has significantly
improved the performance of many natural language processing (NLP) tasks. In
this paper we propose a new model architecture DeBERTa (Decoding-enhanced BERT
with disentangled attention) that improves the BERT and RoBERTa models using
two novel techniques. The first is the disentangled attention mechanism, where
each word is represented using two vectors that encode its content and
position, respectively, and the attention weights among words are computed
using disentangled matrices on their contents and relative positions,
respectively. Second, an enhanced mask decoder is used to incorporate absolute
positions in the decoding layer to predict the masked tokens in model
pre-training. In addition, a new virtual adversarial training method is used
for fine-tuning to improve models' generalization. We show that these
techniques significantly improve the efficiency of model pre-training and the
performance of both natural language understanding (NLU) and natural langauge
generation (NLG) downstream tasks. Compared to RoBERTa-Large, a DeBERTa model
trained on half of the training data performs consistently better on a wide
range of NLP tasks, achieving improvements on MNLI by +0.9% (90.2% vs. 91.1%),
on SQuAD v2.0 by +2.3% (88.4% vs. 90.7%) and RACE by +3.6% (83.2% vs. 86.8%).
Notably, we scale up DeBERTa by training a larger version that consists of 48
Transform layers with 1.5 billion parameters. The significant performance boost
makes the single DeBERTa model surpass the human performance on the SuperGLUE
benchmark (Wang et al., 2019a) for the first time in terms of macro-average
score (89.9 versus 89.8), and the ensemble DeBERTa model sits atop the
SuperGLUE leaderboard as of January 6, 2021, out performing the human baseline
by a decent margin (90.3 versus 89.8).
