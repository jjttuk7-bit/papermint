### - SplatAnything: Gaussian Spacing Normalization for Feed-forward 3DGS across Arbitrary Scenes

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 64 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08264) | [Project](https://zju3dv.github.io/SplatAnything/)

**초록**:

We identify a key limitation of existing feed-forward 3DGS methods: Gaussian attributes are typically predicted in a radial-distance-normalized space that canonicalizes a global radial statistic of the reconstructed geometry, while the spacing between neighboring Gaussian centers can still vary substantially across scene scales and camera configurations. This mismatch induces systematic shifts in Gaussian-scale targets, leading to ambiguous supervision. To address this, we propose SplatAnything with Gaussian Spacing Normalization (GSNorm), which canonicalizes a robust scene-level statistic of Gaussian spacing to provide a more consistent spatial reference for Gaussian prediction. To rigorously assess our method, we further establish a systematic benchmark spanning diverse datasets, input sparsity levels, and challenging extrapolated viewpoints. Extensive experiments demonstrate that SplatAnything achieves state-of-the-art performance, with particularly strong gains under substantial variations in scene scale and viewpoint.

### - Confucius4-R2T2: A Stable-Prefix Streaming ASR Model for Real Real-Time Transcription

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 60 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06043) | [Project](https://r2t2.ai/)

**초록**:

Streaming speech recognition is increasingly consumed by machines, such as translators and voice agents, that act on each token as it appears. A token that is later revised costs wasted work or a wrong action. In this work, we present Confucius4-R2T2 (short for Real Real-Time Transcription), a stable-prefix streaming ASR model that never modifies its earlier output while maintaining high accuracy and low latency. R2T2 is obtained by post-training Qwen3-ASR-1.7B on 1,500 hours of Chinese and English audio while freezing its pre-trained audio encoder and projector. The adaptation is based on the concept of Longest Stable Prefix (LSP), a new notion that we introduce in this work. We prove that LSP is monotone under the prefix order, consistent with the full-audio output, and maximal among labels that equal a whole earlier output. Learning from LSP labels converts a full-context model into a stable-prefix streaming one without any architectural change. Extensive evaluations show that R2T2 brings streaming recognition close to the base model's full-context quality while delivering stable text with low emission lag. Its gains extend beyond the post-training languages, demonstrating a practical path from full-context ASR to stable streaming. We open-source the model weights and code at https://huggingface.co/netease-youdao/Confucius4-R2T2 and https://github.com/netease-youdao/Confucius4-R2T2.

### - P^2-MOPD: Accelerating Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation via Parameter Priors

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 47 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03953)

**초록**:

Multi-teacher on-policy distillation consolidates expert capabilities by supervising student-generated trajectories, whereas task arithmetic offers a training-free alternative through linear parameter combinations. We view the linear combination of student and expert parameters as a single differentiable model and propose P^2-MOPD, which jointly optimizes the mixing coefficients and the student weights under the same on-policy distillation objective. Unlike task arithmetic, which stays within the span of the frozen student and expert parameters for any choice of merging coefficients, P^2-MOPD keeps the student trainable, so the experts act as parameter priors without confining it to this span. Across four domains at 1.7B and 4B scales, P^2-MOPD achieves faster and more balanced recovery than MOPD from the same initialization, surpassing its final average recovery in half the training steps. At the end of training, our 1.7B model reaches 94.7% average recovery, versus 80.9% for MOPD.

### - PCBRouteAgent: How Close Are LLM Agents to Becoming PCB Routing Engineers?

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 35 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08175) | [Project](https://chip-ai-agent.github.io/research/pcbrouteagent/)

**초록**:

Automating printed circuit board (PCB) routing requires coordinating board-level connectivity decisions with geometric execution under strict design rules. Although recent agents can interact with native routing tools, reliable routing on complex boards remains challenging. We present PCBRouteAgent, a multimodal PCB routing agent inspired by how human engineers coordinate global strategy with local execution. A Global Routing Planner combines board images and structured electrical and geometric information to specify connection topology, routing order, corridors, and layer transitions. A Detailed Routing Executor realizes these guides through KiCad's native routing engine and performs bounded local recovery. Unresolved failures are returned as structured feedback, enabling targeted guide revisions while preserving accepted routes. We evaluate PCBRouteAgent on 247 synthetic and real-world boards from four PCBWorld-Bench test splits. It achieves clean-pass rates of 100%, 94.9%, 100%, and 40% on D2, D3-A, D3-B, and D3-C, respectively. On the three real-world subsets, PCBRouteAgent outperforms the best evaluated baseline in clean-pass rate by 13.9, 36, and 10 percentage points, respectively.

### - Toward the Era of Agentic Embodied Intelligence

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 33 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09124) | [Project](https://a2-meas.github.io/)

**초록**:

Foundation models increasingly guide robot actions, yet sustained autonomy requires efficient control and failure recovery. We introduce A2-MEAS, an embodied multi-agent framework built around code as a scaling interface. Learned correction codes combine corrective language, spatial evidence, execution history, and action proposals. MetaBrain provides training supervision and runtime guidance. A correction encoder and adapter absorb this knowledge into policy weights during training, while fresh codes adapt behavior with frozen weights at deployment. The action model, A2-WLA, adds motion-aware history and a latent world model to a vision-language-action policy. Surprisingly, this simple design with action pretraining and task specialization reaches 0.5755 Q-score on the 50-task BEHAVIOR 2026 public subset before online correction, 2.21x the 2025 winner's reported 0.2605 across different benchmark editions and training configurations. On all 100 BEHAVIOR 2026 public tasks, A2-MEAS achieves 0.6411 Q-score and 45.40% task success. On RoboTwin 2.0, A2-WLA achieves 93.98% average success, rising to 95.05% with the complete system. Across 12 real-robot tasks in five household settings, A2-WLA achieves 57.92% average success versus 26.67% for pi0.5. In long-horizon pour-over coffee preparation, a history-conditioned adapter raises post-failure completion at two filter-handling steps from 14% to 94% and from 8% to 82%, compared with A2-WLA retries. Our training infrastructure delivers 427.1 samples per second, 4.03x the throughput of a pi0.5 reference implementation on matched hardware. We will release the system, models, training scripts, and infrastructure to support efficient embodied learning.

### - Extending Context Window of Large Language Models via Positional
  Interpolation

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 55 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.15595)

**초록**:

We present Position Interpolation (PI) that extends the context window sizes
of RoPE-based pretrained LLMs such as LLaMA models to up to 32768 with minimal
fine-tuning (within 1000 steps), while demonstrating strong empirical results
on various tasks that require long context, including passkey retrieval,
language modeling, and long document summarization from LLaMA 7B to 65B.
Meanwhile, the extended model by Position Interpolation preserve quality
relatively well on tasks within its original context window. To achieve this
goal, Position Interpolation linearly down-scales the input position indices to
match the original context window size, rather than extrapolating beyond the
trained context length which may lead to catastrophically high attention scores
that completely ruin the self-attention mechanism. Our theoretical study shows
that the upper bound of interpolation is at least sim 600 times smaller
than that of extrapolation, further demonstrating its stability. Models
extended via Position Interpolation retain its original architecture and can
reuse most pre-existing optimization and infrastructure.

### - Stable Video Diffusion: Scaling Latent Video Diffusion Models to Large
  Datasets

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 15 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.15127)

**초록**:

We present Stable Video Diffusion - a latent video diffusion model for
high-resolution, state-of-the-art text-to-video and image-to-video generation.
Recently, latent diffusion models trained for 2D image synthesis have been
turned into generative video models by inserting temporal layers and finetuning
them on small, high-quality video datasets. However, training methods in the
literature vary widely, and the field has yet to agree on a unified strategy
for curating video data. In this paper, we identify and evaluate three
different stages for successful training of video LDMs: text-to-image
pretraining, video pretraining, and high-quality video finetuning. Furthermore,
we demonstrate the necessity of a well-curated pretraining dataset for
generating high-quality videos and present a systematic curation process to
train a strong base model, including captioning and filtering strategies. We
then explore the impact of finetuning our base model on high-quality data and
train a text-to-video model that is competitive with closed-source video
generation. We also show that our base model provides a powerful motion
representation for downstream tasks such as image-to-video generation and
adaptability to camera motion-specific LoRA modules. Finally, we demonstrate
that our model provides a strong multi-view 3D-prior and can serve as a base to
finetune a multi-view diffusion model that jointly generates multiple views of
objects in a feedforward fashion, outperforming image-based methods at a
fraction of their compute budget. We release code and model weights at
https://github.com/Stability-AI/generative-models .

### - STaR: Bootstrapping Reasoning With Reasoning

**카테고리**: Survey | **upvotes**: 9 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14465)

**초록**:

Generating step-by-step "chain-of-thought" rationales improves language model
performance on complex reasoning tasks like mathematics or commonsense
question-answering. However, inducing language model rationale generation
currently requires either constructing massive rationale datasets or
sacrificing accuracy by using only few-shot inference. We propose a technique
to iteratively leverage a small number of rationale examples and a large
dataset without rationales, to bootstrap the ability to perform successively
more complex reasoning. This technique, the "Self-Taught Reasoner" (STaR),
relies on a simple loop: generate rationales to answer many questions, prompted
with a few rationale examples; if the generated answers are wrong, try again to
generate a rationale given the correct answer; fine-tune on all the rationales
that ultimately yielded correct answers; repeat. We show that STaR
significantly improves performance on multiple datasets compared to a model
fine-tuned to directly predict final answers, and performs comparably to
fine-tuning a 30times larger state-of-the-art language model on
CommensenseQA. Thus, STaR lets a model improve itself by learning from its own
generated reasoning.
